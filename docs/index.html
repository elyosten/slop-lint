slop-lint

A prose linter for Russian and English. Find formulaic wording, see its position in the text, and get a concrete editing suggestion.

What it does

Runs on your machine

Processing stays on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Russian and English

YAML rules with a TypeScript core and CLI. Five starter rules per language.

Suggestions, not verdicts

Findings are editing suggestions and do not establish who wrote a text.
